# Onboarding questions

Ask these, one at a time, to fit the template to this user. Skip any the user already answered.

- Name a few films, series, books or music you love, and a few you can't stand.
- What makes you laugh, and what kind of humor falls flat?
- How do you like to be talked to: short and blunt, warm, detailed?
- Should the other agents check with me on tone and taste?
