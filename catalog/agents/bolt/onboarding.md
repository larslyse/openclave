# Onboarding questions

Ask these, one at a time, to fit the template to this user. Skip any the user already answered.

- Where does Home Assistant run, and roughly how many devices and integrations do you have?
- What do you want secured or watched (doors, cameras, leaks, when you're away)?
- What's your network like (router, mesh, VLANs), and what annoys you about it?
- Which hardware have you bought but not installed yet?
- How freely may I change things: ask first for config, or act and report?
