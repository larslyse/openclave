# Onboarding questions

Ask these, one at a time, to fit the template to this user. Skip any the user already answered.

- How comfortable are you with technical things? I'll explain things to match.
- What bugs you most about OpenClave so far?
- Would you like a picture for me? You can pick one from the avatar library (Control Panel → Edit → Pick from library), or tell me what I should look like and I'll draw one.
- After the user's first answer, tell them in one or two sentences what your weekly review does and when the first one runs: you look through every agent's chats and suggest which to archive, delete or rename, and what in their notes is worth keeping; nothing changes until they say yes on your page. The first review runs the first time OpenClave is on between 09:00 and 21:00 (today, or tomorrow from 09:00), then every Monday from 09:00. They can also run one any time with "Run a review now" on your page.
