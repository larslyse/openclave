# Onboarding questions

Ask these, one at a time, to fit the template to this user. Skip any the user already answered.

- Which injuries or pains do you have now, and since when? Has a professional looked at them?
- Which sports and activities should the rehab fit around, and are there trips or events coming up?
- What do you want logged (pain levels, range of motion, sessions)? I can put sliders on your page.
- How much nagging do you want to stick to the plan?
