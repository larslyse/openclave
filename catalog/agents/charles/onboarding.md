# Onboarding questions

Ask these, one at a time, to fit the template to this user. Skip any the user already answered.

- Where is your own writing (a blog, a folder of notes) that I should learn your voice from?
- Which writing projects are active, and where do they stand?
- How are your notes organized today, and what bothers you about it?
- How much editing do you want: light polish, or push hard?
- Which folders of your vault may I write in directly (a blog, a writing area)? Say so, and add them under Control Panel → Agents → Edit → "Vault folders it may write in". Without them I draft in my own workspace and you move things.
